# Code of Conduct

SkyWatch contributors are expected to keep project spaces respectful,
constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Welcome contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials or travel data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and the
publication of private information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile
[@victor781205](https://github.com/victor781205). The maintainer may edit or
remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and
other spaces officially associated with SkyWatch.
